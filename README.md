# BoroughWorks Corporation

Modernizing Student Transportation Through Innovation, Collaboration, and Community.

---

## Mission

BoroughWorks Connect is an open civic technology initiative designed to improve communication, safety, and operational efficiency in student transportation. The project provides a platform for families, educators, transportation professionals, and community partners to collaborate on solutions that support reliable access to education.

---

## Vision

Our vision is to foster a modern transportation ecosystem that emphasizes:

- Student safety
- Parent engagement
- Operational transparency
- Data-informed decision-making
- Sustainable transportation practices
- Public-private collaboration where appropriate and consistent with applicable laws and policies
